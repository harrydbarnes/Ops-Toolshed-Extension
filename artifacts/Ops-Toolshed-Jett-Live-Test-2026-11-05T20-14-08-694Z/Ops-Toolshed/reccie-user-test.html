Live per-user writer test

This pilot uses the live Reccie Tracker and its existing save records. Choose one placement you have genuinely reconciled for the first test.

  1. Open Reccies and expand Reccie tracking settings.
  2. Choose Connect my Google account, select your own work account and review Google’s requested permissions. Return to Reccies after Google authorisation finishes.
  3. Choose Check access. Check the email shown and the access results for the tracker and save-record spreadsheet.
  4. When both sheets are accessible, choose Use my Google account for updates. This pauses automatic updates for your first test and requires your own account for future writer requests, including requests from older builds.
  5. Verify one genuinely reconciled placement. Use Mark Yes, review the preview and confirm the save.
  6. Check the saved value and its Google Sheets cell edit history. The new edit should show your email.

If access is missing, ask Harry to share the save-record spreadsheet with your work account as an Editor. Editors can read and change the records directly in Google Sheets.

Record the connected email, access result, D number, save confirmation and cell edit history for Harry. Share these internally.
