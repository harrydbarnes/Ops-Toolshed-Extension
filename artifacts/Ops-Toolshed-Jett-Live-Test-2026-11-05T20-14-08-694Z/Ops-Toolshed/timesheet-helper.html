Timesheet Helper

Keep a weekly draft, then review and enter it in Production. Prisma activity is an estimate, not a record of all work.

Prisma activity

When enabled, the focused Prisma campaign is sampled after mouse or keyboard activity. Background tabs are excluded; sampling stops after 60 seconds without interaction. Client and product codes and daily activity totals stay on this device. Features must be on for tracking.

Your week

Open Production

Choose any date to open its Monday–Sunday week. Hours and mappings are saved locally. Read the matching open Production week to add visible rows and non-zero hours without replacing draft entries. Add meetings, leave and other work manually.

Rows are your draft, not a live copy of Production. Check account codes and the week against your Production template.

Estimate client hours

Enter actual total hours for a day, including overtime. The helper subtracts all hours already entered, then divides the remainder using that day’s recorded Prisma activity. Review the results before applying them.

Client mappings

Map each observed Prisma client/product to the appropriate Production account code from the read-only client-code workbook. The correct code may depend on your team. Unmapped activity is never allocated.

Open client-code workbook

Friday office closure

Summer dates are year-specific. Set them for the selected week’s year; Friday suggests 2.5 hours during that period and 1.5 otherwise. Check leave and exceptions yourself.

Transfer to Production

Review the week and account codes above. Copy a tab-separated summary for reference, then enter the values in Production and use its Save and Submit buttons. Check existing entries first; this helper does not write to Production.
