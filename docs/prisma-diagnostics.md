# Prisma diagnostics measurement contract

Diagnostics use the shared loading monitor, not network interception or synthetic requests. Recognised selectors are `#vp-block > i.fa.fa-circle-o-notch.fa-spin`, `mo-spinner`, `.mo-spinner`, `svg.spinner`, and `mo-skeleton.mo-grid-skeleton`. Side-panel ancestry is `mo-side-panel`, `.mo-side-panel`, or `.workflow-widget-wrapper`, including shadow hosts. A continuous union of visible indicators is one interval per page/side-panel scope. Swapping overlapping indicators does not restart the interval. Disappearance means the observed indicator interval completed, not that the route is ready.

Placement timing begins on an enabled `#btn-save` or `#btn-save-and-add-another` inside a placement-labelled form/dialog. Only changed/new explicit placement success or placement error notices terminate it. Existing notices and unrelated errors are ignored. Missing acknowledgement times out after 20 seconds. This is placement-save evidence, not attribution of financial Pending operations.

Route changes and pagehide interrupt observations. Indicator timeout is 300 seconds, emitted once until indicators disappear. Disabling discards active observations. Reset is serialized with background tracking writes and uses a cutoff to reject delayed pre-reset samples. Reset discards active diagnostics and starts fresh indicator observation. Abrupt process termination cannot guarantee a final message; no success is inferred. Duplicate identical samples are ignored while present in the recent ring. Transport/storage failures are logged, not retried (to avoid duplicating aggregates).

Only timestamp (UTC milliseconds), allowlisted signal and area, bounded duration (milliseconds), and allowlisted outcome cross the diagnostics storage boundary. Extra fields are rejected. Financial Pending, perceived slowness and slow/incomplete renders are manual observations with zero duration and `reported` outcome; they are excluded from latency percentiles. No route-specific readiness or financial completion instrumentation is claimed.

Storage key: `prismaDiagnostics`. Up to 366 UTC date buckets (rolling 365-day cutoff), each with at most 6 signals Ã— 8 areas, 11 fixed histogram buckets and outcome counters; latest 1,000 sanitised samples. At worst populated across every group/day, JSON should remain below roughly 6 MB plus a ~160 KB sample ring, within Chrome local storage's 10 MB limit, but other extension data shares that quota. A fully populated example with six-digit counters measured 4,558,771 JSON bytes including the sample ring. Storage failures return an error. Histogram bounds: 250, 500, 1000, 2000, 5000, 10000, 20000, 30000, 60000, 120000, 300000 ms. Percentiles use the containing bucket's upper bound (estimates); slow is >=10 seconds. Interrupted/error/timeout durations are included and outcomes are shown separately.

The Stats period controls filter UTC day keys, including exports; manual-report area does not filter charts. Export reconstructs allowlisted aggregate fields and revalidates recent samples, includes histogram bounds and extension version/build, and never includes existing campaign stats. Reset clears diagnostics along with existing stats. No data is transmitted. The existing Stats Collector setting gates both collector and background writes.

Live host verification is required before extending selectors or claiming full readiness, blank-page detection or financial Pending attribution.
