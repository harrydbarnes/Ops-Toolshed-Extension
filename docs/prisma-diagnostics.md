# Prisma diagnostics measurement contract

Diagnostics use the shared loading monitor, not network interception or synthetic requests. Recognised selectors are `#vp-block > i.fa.fa-circle-o-notch.fa-spin`, `mo-spinner`, `.mo-spinner`, `svg.spinner`, and `mo-skeleton.mo-grid-skeleton`. Side-panel ancestry is `mo-side-panel`, `.mo-side-panel`, or `.workflow-widget-wrapper`, including shadow hosts. A continuous union of visible indicators is one interval per page/side-panel scope. Swapping overlapping indicators does not restart the interval. Disappearance means the observed indicator interval completed, not that the route is ready.

Placement timing begins on an enabled `#btn-save` or `#btn-save-and-add-another` inside a placement-labelled form/dialog. Only changed/new explicit placement success or placement error notices terminate it. Existing notices and unrelated errors are ignored. Missing acknowledgement times out after 20 seconds. This is placement-save evidence, not attribution of financial Pending operations.

Route changes, pagehide and hiding the tab interrupt observations. Hidden tabs cannot start indicator timings. After returning, indicators must be observed idle before a new interval starts, so a load that spans a hidden period cannot become a misleading completed timing. Placement acknowledgement watching continues after timing is interrupted: a real success can still increment the existing confirmed-save count without contributing completed latency. Indicator timeout is 300 seconds, emitted once until indicators disappear. Disabling discards active observations. Reset is serialized with background tracking writes and uses a cutoff to reject delayed pre-reset samples. Reset discards active diagnostics and starts fresh indicator observation. Abrupt process termination cannot guarantee a final message; no success is inferred. Duplicate identical samples are ignored while present in the recent ring. Transport/storage failures are logged, not retried (to avoid duplicating aggregates).

Only timestamp (UTC milliseconds), allowlisted signal and area, bounded duration (milliseconds), and allowlisted outcome cross the diagnostics storage boundary. Extra fields are rejected. Financial Pending, perceived slowness and slow/incomplete renders are manual observations with zero duration and `reported` outcome; they are excluded from latency percentiles. No route-specific readiness or financial completion instrumentation is claimed.

Storage key: `prismaDiagnostics`. Up to 366 UTC date buckets (rolling 365-day cutoff), each with at most 6 signals × 12 areas, two sets of 11 fixed histogram buckets and outcome counters; latest 1,000 sanitised samples. The extra categories increase the worst-case aggregate size: a fully populated synthetic example is approximately 10 MB including the sample ring, close to Chrome local storage's 10 MB limit. Actual manual-report histograms remain empty, but other extension data shares that quota. Storage failures return an error. Histogram bounds: 250, 500, 1000, 2000, 5000, 10000, 20000, 30000, 60000, 120000, 300000 ms. Percentiles use the containing bucket's upper bound (estimates); slow completions take >=10 seconds.

`histogram` and `slow` retain the original all-outcome aggregates for compatibility. New `completedHistogram` and `completedSlow` fields exclusively measure completed observations and supply the chart. Interrupted/error/timeout observations retain their outcome counts and recent samples, but never enter completed latency. Completed timing coverage is shown separately from total sample and completed-outcome counts. Days without usable completed timings show no plotted point and a dash in the table, not a zero duration.

Legacy groups containing only completed observations can retain their existing histogram. Mixed legacy groups keep counts/outcomes but supply no historical latency: the capped recent ring cannot reliably reconstruct the full aggregate. The first new append starts a separate completed histogram without erasing old counts. Previously collected completed-only history may include background time; that cannot be reconstructed retrospectively.

The Stats period controls filter UTC day keys, including exports; manual-report area does not filter charts. Export schema version 2 declares `timingBasis: completed`, reconstructs allowlisted aggregate fields (including the completed histogram when available), revalidates recent samples, includes histogram bounds and extension version/build, and never includes existing campaign stats. Reset clears diagnostics along with existing stats. No data is transmitted. The existing Stats Collector setting gates both collector and background writes.

Live host verification is required before extending selectors or claiming full readiness, blank-page detection or financial Pending attribution.
