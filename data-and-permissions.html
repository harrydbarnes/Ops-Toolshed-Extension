Data and permissions

See what is saved, where connections go and why browser access is needed.

Where your data lives

Chrome profile

Saved in your Chrome profile

Feature choices, setup profile, reminder schedule and custom reminder text.

May sync when Chrome Sync is enabled and the extension ID matches. Control in Settings and Chrome Sync.

This browser

Local extension storage

Campaign history, approval records, approver favourites and recipient history, Stats, diagnostics and saved finance references.

Clear or disable these in the relevant tool.

Your files

Exports you choose

Uploaded reports are processed in the extension page. Saved references may stay locally; exported results and diagnostics go to your download location.

Clear source data in the tool; delete exports when no longer needed.

When data leaves the extension

Mediaocean Prisma features
Uses your signed-in session for campaign actions and approval checks. Enabled approval tracking polls every five minutes for Prisma bookers.
Company SharePoint Help Guides
Opens company guidance and loads guide PDFs.
Meta Graph API Optional checker
Uses your supplied access token to retrieve Meta data. The token is stored locally as a recoverable value, without extension encryption. Remove it in Social Booking Checker.
Public font & style CDNs Page appearance
Receive ordinary request metadata, such as your IP address. Extension code does not send campaign records to these services.

There is no separate analytics endpoint. Stats and diagnostics stay local unless you export them. Diagnostics Mode expires after 24 hours or a browser restart and can be cleared sooner.

What browser access supports

Preferences & reminders
Storage, alarms and notifications save choices and show reminders.
Page tools & navigation
Active tab, tabs, scripting and side panel support page enhancements, shortcuts and guides.
Copy & paste actions
Clipboard read/write and offscreen access support explicit copy and paste. Clipboard content can be sensitive and is handled for the requested action.
Service connections
Host access covers Mediaocean sites, company SharePoint and Meta Graph API.

Choosing “I don't book on Prisma” pauses Prisma injection and approval polling. It preserves preferences and existing local records; it does not remove Chrome permissions.

Use a company-managed Chrome profile and follow company retention rules for exported files. This overview supports rollout review and does not replace company data policy.
